# Website content conventions

## Publication venues

- Use one venue format everywhere: `Official Venue Name (ACRONYM) YYYY`.
- If the official public name is already an acronym or brand, use `Venue YYYY`.
- For workshops, use `Workshop Name Workshop YYYY`.
- Do not put a comma before the year and do not use `@` in publication venue labels.
- Keep non-venue labels in title case, for example `World Labs Tech Report` and `In Preparation`.

Examples:

- `Robotics: Science and Systems (RSS) 2026`
- `Dex-CoRL Workshop 2026`

## Community service

- Group all conference and workshop reviewing into one line: `Reviewer: VENUE YY, VENUE YY`.
- Use venue acronyms and two-digit years in the reviewer line.
- Do not create a separate entry or horizontal divider for each reviewing role.
- Keep organizing roles as separate entries, but do not place horizontal dividers between Community Service entries.

## WUDI public mirror

- `/wudi/` is the public, non-anonymous mirror of the separate double-blind WUDI site.
- Keep the public mirror's author list and `Dex-CoRL Workshop 2026` venue visible.
- Do not copy the anonymous site's `Anonymous Authors` or `Under review · ICRA 2027` labels into the public mirror.
- Do not modify or commit the separate anonymous WUDI repository while updating this website.
