<section class="project-story">
  <figure><img src="{{ '/images/bee3900/robot-arm.webp' | relative_url }}" alt="Bio Robot operating its crop-harvesting arm" loading="lazy" decoding="async"><figcaption>Perception and manipulation on the harvesting robot.</figcaption></figure>
  <div><h2>See, navigate, harvest</h2><p>An autonomous crop-harvesting robot that follows planted rows, detects crops, classifies plant condition, and uses a robotic arm for pruning or collection.</p><p>A Raspberry Pi handles camera input, object detection, classification, and arm control. An Arduino manages motor control, line following, and sensor feedback.</p><h3>Mobile manipulation</h3><p>Four mecanum wheels provide omnidirectional motion for alignment with crops. A three-channel line tracker, IMU, and limit switches support navigation, while a Le Robot SO-101 arm positions the claw for manipulation.</p></div>
</section>

  <div><h2>Perception in the loop</h2><p>An Innomaker camera with LED-assisted illumination supplies images to YOLOv8 Nano for plant detection and MobileNetV3 for classification of healthy and unhealthy crops.</p><p>The project brings embedded control, mobility, vision, and arm manipulation together in a complete harvesting workflow.</p></div>
</section>
