<section class="project-story">
  <div class="bio-grass-comparison"><figure><img src="{{ '/images/bee3900/good-grass.webp' | relative_url }}" alt="Healthy crop example" loading="lazy" decoding="async"><figcaption>Healthy crop</figcaption></figure><figure><img src="{{ '/images/bee3900/bad-grass.webp' | relative_url }}" alt="Unhealthy crop example" loading="lazy" decoding="async"><figcaption>Unhealthy crop</figcaption></figure></div>
  <div><h2>Perception in the loop</h2><p>An Innomaker camera with LED-assisted illumination supplies images to YOLOv8 Nano for plant detection and MobileNetV3 for classification of healthy and unhealthy crops.</p><p>A Raspberry Pi handles camera input, object detection, classification, and arm control. An Arduino manages motor control, line following, and sensor feedback.</p></div>
</section>

<section class="project-story project-story--reverse">
  <figure><a href="{{ '/images/bee3900/poster.webp' | relative_url }}"><img src="{{ '/images/bee3900/poster.webp' | relative_url }}" alt="Bio Robot project poster with system design and results" loading="lazy" decoding="async"></a><figcaption>Project poster — click to view at full size.</figcaption></figure>
  <div><h2>See, navigate, harvest</h2><p>An autonomous crop-harvesting robot that follows planted rows, detects crops, classifies plant condition, and uses a robotic arm for pruning or collection.</p><p>Four mecanum wheels provide omnidirectional motion for alignment with crops. A three-channel line tracker, IMU, and limit switches support navigation, while a Le Robot SO-101 arm positions the claw for manipulation.</p></div>
</section>
