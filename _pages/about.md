I earned my B.S. in Computer Science with honors from [Cornell University](https://www.cornell.edu/), with a minor in Robotics. I spent my senior year as a visiting student at [UC Berkeley](https://www.berkeley.edu/), working with Professor [Koushil Sreenath](https://hybrid-robotics.berkeley.edu/koushil/).
{: .intro}

Previously, I worked with Professor [Tapomayukh Bhattacharjee](https://sites.google.com/site/tapomayukh) on robotic assistive caregiving. I also interned at [SceniX](https://scenix.ai/) ([World Labs](https://www.worldlabs.ai/)), working with [Yunzhu Li](https://yunzhuli.github.io/) on Real2Sim2Real for robot learning.
{: .intro}

My research lies at the intersection of robotics, learning, and control. I focus on enabling robots to perform useful tasks by combining novel hardware design with learning-based methods that scale with data.
